What Is a Packout?

A packout is the careful removal, protection, and inventory of belongings so restoration or rebuild work can move forward. Contents are often removed to allow drying, demolition, odor control, or safer access for crews. When your space is ready, items are returned and placed with care.

Our Packout-to-Packback Process

1

On-Site Assessment & Plan

We review affected areas, identify what stays versus what must be removed, and outline a clear plan.

2

Room-by-Room Packing & Protection

Items are wrapped, boxed, labeled, and protected to prevent damage and keep rooms organized.

3

Inventory & Tracking

We document contents with photos and labels and maintain a clear chain-of-custody process.

4

Transport to Storage or Cleaning

Contents are transported to secure storage or coordinated cleaning as needed.

5

Storage Until Ready

Items stay protected and organized until restoration or rebuild work is complete.

6

Pack-Back & Placement

We return, unwrap, and place items to help you get back to normal quickly.

FAQ

How long does a packout take?

Timing depends on the size of the property and scope, but most packouts are completed within a day or two.

Can I access items while they are stored?

Yes. We can arrange scheduled access to retrieve important items as needed.

What should I take with me immediately?

We recommend gathering daily essentials, medications, and valuables before work begins.

How do you track items?

Items are labeled, photographed, and documented to maintain a clear inventory and chain of custody.

When does pack-back happen?

Pack-back starts once the restoration or rebuild is complete and the space is safe to move back in.

Do you handle partial packouts?

Yes. We can pack out only the rooms or items needed for the work area.
